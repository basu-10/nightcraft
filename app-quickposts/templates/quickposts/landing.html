Workflow

How a post gets made

01

Ingest

Paste your text

An article, a listicle draft, talking points — any block of text. QuickPost tokenizes and segments it into logical slide boundaries automatically.

02

Generate

Slides become images

Each segment renders as a formatted slide image with your brand's typography, color palette, and aspect ratio. Export all at once.

03

Refine

Polish each asset

Run slides through Quick Edits to apply crops, filters, watermarks, or annotations. Batch-apply changes across all frames for consistency.

Publish

Compose and ship

Arrange refined assets into a listicle grid, scrapbook layout, or carousel. Export at the exact dimensions your platform requires — ready to post.
