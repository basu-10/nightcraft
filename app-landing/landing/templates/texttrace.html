Principles

Search that respects
where your data lives

TextTrace is built around a few firm beliefs about personal search and the machines it runs on.

01

Runs On Your Machine

Embeddings are computed locally with a small model. Nothing leaves your device — no API calls, no cloud round-trips, no accounts.

02

Meaning, Not Matching

Vector search ranks by similarity of meaning. Ask in your own words and find the paragraph that says it differently.

03

Portable Indexes

Your index is a folder of plain vectors and metadata. Back it up, move it, inspect it — no proprietary lock-in.

How it works

From text to traceable meaning

Four quiet steps, all of them offline.

01

Index

Point TextTrace at a folder of notes, PDFs, or code. It reads them in the background and chunks them into passages.

02

Embed

A small on-device model turns each passage into a vector. The work happens on your CPU — no network required.

03

Store

Vectors and metadata land in a local, portable index you own. Re-index incrementally as files change.

04

Retrieve

Type a question. TextTrace returns the closest passages with similarity scores, so you can trust the ranking.

Features

What's under the hood

Focused on the essentials of fast, private, semantic search.

Semantic Search

Find passages by intent, not exact keywords. Relevance is scored by vector similarity, shown as a clear percentage.

Local Embeddings

A compact model runs entirely on-device. No API keys, no usage limits, no data sent anywhere.

Private by Design

Your files never leave the machine. Search history stays local and is cleared on demand.

Multiple Indexes

Keep separate indexes for notes, research, and code. Switch contexts without mixing results.

PDF & Plain Text

Index Markdown, plain text, and PDFs out of the box. More formats arrive as the app matures.

Portable Output

Export results and indexes as standard files. Your data stays usable even if you stop using the app.
